Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Palette Studio ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Palette Studio Android mobile application (com.palettestudioo.app).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Cloud Sync Information: Signing in is optional. If you choose to sign in with Google (via Firebase Authentication), we receive your basic profile details—such as your name, email address, and profile photo—to authenticate your account and sync your saved palettes and preferences across devices via Cloud Firestore.
  • User-Created Content: Color palettes, gradients, favourites, and studio settings you create are stored locally on your device and, when signed in, synced securely to your cloud account.
  • Camera, Photos & Media Access: When you use features such as Camera Color Extraction, Image Color Picker, or Glass Studio, the app requests access to your device camera (CAMERA) or photos you explicitly select. All colour extraction and image/wallpaper processing happen locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or view your personal photos or camera feed on our servers.
  • Wallpaper & Local Exports: When you export wallpapers or apply them directly (SET_WALLPAPER), generated images are saved locally to your device storage or set as your wallpaper at your request.
  • Purchase & Subscription Data: In-app purchases (including subscriptions and one-time Pro purchases) are processed securely by Google Play Billing and managed via RevenueCat using an anonymous or app-linked user identifier. We do not collect or store your credit card, UPI, or banking details.
  • Diagnostics & Performance: Basic technical and crash diagnostics handled by the Android platform or Google Play Services may be used to maintain app stability and fix bugs. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or sell your personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core studio features including palette generation, gradient & mesh creation, optical glass effects, and high-resolution exports.
  • To back up, restore, and sync your saved palettes across Android devices when you sign in with Google.
  • To verify, restore, and unlock Pro subscriptions and purchases.
  • To maintain security, prevent fraud, and improve overall app performance.

3. Third-Party Services

Palette Studio relies on trusted third-party services that process data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore
  • RevenueCat (Subscription & entitlement management)

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Local palettes remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Cloud-synced data remains active while your account is active. You can delete your saved palettes directly inside the app, or request complete deletion of your account and all associated cloud-synced data (Firebase Authentication record and Cloud Firestore palettes/preferences) by emailing us at the contact address below. Verified deletion requests are processed within 7–30 days. Purchase records required for legal or billing compliance remain managed by Google Play.

5. Children's Privacy

Palette Studio is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, privacy concerns, or account deletion requests, please contact us at: support@palettestudio.app
