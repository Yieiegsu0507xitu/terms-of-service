Palette Studio Privacy Policy

Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how users of Palette Studio (com.palettestudioo.app) can request deletion of their Google Sign-In account data and cloud-synced items.

How to Request Account & Cloud Data Deletion

  1. Send an email to support@palettestudio.app from the Google email address associated with your Palette Studio account.
  2. Include the subject line "Account and Data Deletion Request - Palette Studio" and confirm that you want your cloud-synced account data deleted.
  3. Your deletion request is processed within 7 business days.

Data Deleted vs. Data Retained

  • Deleted upon request: Cloud-synced user palettes, saved design presets, and account profile/sync records linked to your sign-in ID.
  • Local device data: Items stored locally on your device remain until you clear app data or uninstall the app.
  • Retained where required: Purchase and subscription transaction records processed via Google Play Billing / RevenueCat may be retained as required for tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention compliance.

Contact

For questions about account or data deletion, contact: support@palettestudio.app
